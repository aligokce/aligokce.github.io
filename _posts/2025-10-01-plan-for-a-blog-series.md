Starting with the dread of digital note taking in the age of information, I explored many things that I've found to be taken for granted on sharing knowledge and creating experiences that are valued and important for humanity. 

I realise that it may seem like a bunch of serious-looking keywords put together into a salad or pudding. I assure you, it will not. I may change the outline into a very different one. However, the supporting ideas in this outline will most probably prevail.

## Outline

1. Thoughts on Knowledge Management Systems
2. Ownership and the Digital Landscape
3. Standards Bring People Together
4. How We Experience Information
5. Information as a Living Organism
6. Mathematics of Infinities and Impossibilities
7. A New Theory of Everything

## Extensive outline

1. Thoughts on Knowledge Management Systems
    - Lost on Memories: Narrow-mindedness of Suggestion Algorithms
    - Missing in Interaction: World Building and Terminology (Toolset)
    - Failure to Communicate: Communities, Privacy and Open Standards
    - Joy (and Dread) of Possessions: Digital Properties and Value
    - Layers: Compression, Expansion and Complexity In-between
2. Ownership and the Digital Landscape
    - Debt, Property and Money: Action Over Inaction
    - YouTube, Bitcoin and Roblox: Immerse in Virtuality 
    - Credit Where Due: The Simplest Constitution
    - Transfer and Store: Then Banks, Now Servers?
    - Privacy and Inheritance: How Do We Ensure Anything
3. Standards Bring People Together
    - Safety First: Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs
    - Language: The First Standard, Book of Founders
    - Routines and Rituals: Spreading the Knowledge
    - Coherence, Collaboration and Accumulation: Power of Exponentials
    - Missionaries and Evangelists: A Fight Till Death
        - Leaders, Entrepreneurs, Founders

4. How We Experience Information
    - Complexity Paradox and Compression Theory
    - What creates uniqueness? Answer: Randomness.
    - Why journalists use 6W's Questions? Law enforcers? Who else?
    - Working environment of an investigator
    - Question of Semantics and Metadata within an Unknown Context
5. Information as a Living Organism
    - Tree and Roots: Arborescent and Rhizomatic
    - Evolution: Reward/loss mechanism and Antifragility
    - Accessibility in Privacy: DNA and Seed (Initial state and vector)
    - Compression: Interaction, Exploration, Experimentation and Creation (Public → Private)
    - Expansion: Collection, Preservation, Presentation and Collaboration (Private → Public)
6. Mathematics of Infinities and Impossibilities
    - Scales and Separation of Interaction: Quantum Wave?
    - Physical and Logical Impossibilities: Intuition Slides Away
    - Computational Impossibilities: Kolmogorov's Complexity Paradox
    - Cryptographic Impossibilities: Privacy in Plain Sight
    - The Meta-impossibility: Gödel's Incompleteness Theorem
7. A New Theory of Everything
    - Cycles and Chaos: Two Poles and Three Body Problem:
    - Tree and Forest: Complex Systems Theory
    - Strings and Knots: Noumena and Phenomena
        - Wave, Quantum and Time Horizon (where infinity happens)
    - Semantics at Different Scales and Uncertainty Principle
    - Problem of Knowing the Initial State: The Ultimate Secret All Around Us
