# Line Following Robot

2015-03-01

* * *

When I was studying in English Preparatory School at METU, a team of curious prep students collaborated to design a line-following robot - without any previous knowledge beforehand. We started from scratch to explore the world of makers. Below is a photo of an early prototype of a line following robot design of our own.



{% include elements/figure.html image="line_following_2.jpeg" caption="Photo: A very early prototype of the project" %}



Unfortunately, any photo of the final product does not exist, but a video is available.



Video: Our design, Sigmar, racing in the line following category at 13th International METU Robots Days.

[Sigmar: Line-following robot](https://www.youtube.com/embed/_YepcsIzAK0?feature=oembed "Sigmar: Line-following robot")
{% include elements/video.html id="_YepcsIzAK0" %}


Our curiosity made us learn about many different concepts, from handling sensor data, Arduino programming, various control methods like [PID](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PID_controller), designing a proper chassis using SOLIDWORKS, controlling a CNC machine, _et cetera_.



{% include elements/figure.html image="line_following_1.jpeg" caption="Figure: Chassis design with the component placement planning in Solidworks" %}
