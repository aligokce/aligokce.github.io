# Concert at a non-profit event

2019-04-21

METU Alumni Association Visnelik Hall, Ankara

* * *

This was a non-profit for-children event special for April 23rd National Sovereignty and Children's Day, named Anadolu'dan Bir Demet Çiçek Project, organised by ODTÜ İLKYAR non-profit organisation.

I played in first violins within Ankara Young Philharmonic Orchestra and Polyphonic Choir (AGFO).
